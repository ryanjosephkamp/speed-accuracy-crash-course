Waiting, weightedA crash course in the paper

How the best moment to decide moves when time gets more expensive, worked through one result at a time on a cost schedule of your own. The figures are computed in the page from the paper's equations, and a check script holds the numbers to the paper's.

Working draft. Chapter 6 is the first chapter built; the others follow.

Your schedule

Pick a schedule for the cost of time, or draw one. The book keeps it and uses it in every chapter. Under it is the best bound for your schedule, solved in this page by dynamic programming, beside the per-moment rule's bound.
